# Overview

Muscle Map is a workout tracker that helps people see which muscles they have trained and how hard. Most workout trackers emphasize numbers; Muscle Map makes the muscles themselves the focus.

## Who it's for
Muscle Map is for people who want to understand their training at a glance, track their workouts, and see how their training is distributed across their muscles.

## The problem
Use SetGraph as the reference app.
- Where it's good: clean set rows, fast entry.
- Where it falls short: create and open look the same; no muscle picture; effort isn't tracked; supersets are behind a paywall.

## Core loop
Create a workout → start it (Lift) → look back at what you did (history and the muscle map).

## Features by tab
- **Home — Planned:** A muscle map, training streak, goal rings, and workout history.
- **Body — Prototype:** On sample data. Shows an anatomy figure shaded according to sets, plus stats and insight cards.
- **Lift — Built:** Search, saved workouts with UP NEXT, set entry with the plate loader, and a rest timer with notifications.
- **Goals — Planned:** Goals for workout frequency, volume, and strength.
- **Camera — Planned, stretch goal.**

## Open product questions
